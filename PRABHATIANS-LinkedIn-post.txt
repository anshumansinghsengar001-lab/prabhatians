I’m excited to share PRABHATIANS — a peer-learning platform for students at Prabhat Engineering College.

The idea is simple: Connect · Learn · Share. Students can build a learning profile, share what they know, discover courses and peers, and track their learning progress.

I’ve built the responsive Next.js frontend and a TypeScript/Express API backed by MongoDB, with JWT authentication and APIs for courses, skills, search, enrollment, comments, ratings, saved content, notifications, and progress.
